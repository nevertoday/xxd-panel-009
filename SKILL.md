# XXD Panel 009 · 极简网点空间版画

Turn each supplied photograph into a finished quiet screenprint poster. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must reduce the same source to one uniquely recognisable visual anchor, choose one direction and one spatial relation, and let paper-toned negative space carry most of the image. Two or three spot colours, halftone density instead of digital blur, and intelligent editorial type create distance, stillness, passage, echo, boundary, time, light, or trace without becoming a generic retro template.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, silhouette, pose, action, and narrative relation through at least three source-specific cues while compressing complexity into one small unique visual anchor, one direction, and one spatial relation. It is not tracing, a detailed illustration, a multi-focus collage, a decorative retro template, glossy vector art, photoreal painting, or 3D.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 009 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve identity, silhouette, pose, action, and at least three defining cues → compress all complexity into one uniquely recognisable anchor → choose exactly one dominant direction and one source-earned spatial relation → keep the anchor small and make paper-toned negative space the principal field → organise two or three spot colours as paper base, main ink, and optional mist layer → express fog, distance, shadow, fading, and transition only through halftone dots that move naturally from dense to sparse → retain paper grain, screenprint ink, slight misregistration, and uneven inking → integrate one very short title plus at most three micro-editorial elements into horizon, contour, axis, halftone boundary, or negative shape**.

The poetry comes from the source's real relation between subject, direction, distance, boundary, depth, stillness, and movement—not from stock loneliness symbols, fake archival labels, or a reusable art-book layout. Screenprint, modernist editorial design, architecture journals, independent exhibitions, and artist books are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the anchor, dominant direction, spatial relation, halftone transition, colour temperature, or copy. Also reject several focal points, combined composition tricks, large subjects that consume the field, detailed tracing, dense scenery, ordinary gradients, digital blur, cheap ageing filters, arbitrary labels, large advertising headlines, glossy vectors, photoreal shading, 3D volume, and decoration not earned by the source.

## 009 visual system

### One small, uniquely recognisable anchor

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Compress the principal subject or inseparable relation into one decisive anchor with very few edges and no rich illustration detail. It is usually small in the frame, yet preserves identity, action, function, emotion, and the most important spatial relationship.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Keep one principal subject or inseparable relationship. A horizon, boundary, shadow, or halftone band may support it only when it clarifies distance, direction, depth, or isolation.

### One direction, one spatial relation

- Select exactly one structure earned by the photograph: horizontal extension, vertical extension, isolated suspension, outward dissipation, or shallow-to-deep recession. Do not combine several merely to appear designed.
- Use scale, alignment, a single horizon, a boundary, one shadow, or one halftone band to establish that relation. Blank space is active distance, air, pause, or time—not leftover background.
- Place the anchor with clear directional tension and a deliberate relationship to the canvas edge. Preserve one readable route through the composition.
- Keep detail low, identity high, and the spatial proposition singular. No dense scene reconstruction, diagrammatic explanation, or scattered motifs.

### Halftone-only atmosphere and physical print

- Build every fog bank, depth cue, shadow, fade, dissipation, and spatial transition with visible halftone dots whose density naturally moves from dense to sparse. Never use digital blur or a smooth ordinary gradient.
- Keep colour shapes flat and allow paper grain, screenprint ink, slight registration shift, incomplete coverage, and subtly uneven inking to remain visible.
- Halftone scale and density must support the chosen distance relation without swallowing the small anchor or filling all negative space.
- Reject airbrush softness, Gaussian blur, gradient meshes, realistic lighting, complex volume, polished vector edges, plastic CGI, and indiscriminate distressed overlays.

### Paper base, main ink, mist layer

- Derive overall temperature, value, and emotion from the photograph without directly copying its palette. Use two or three spot colours: off-white, warm grey, or natural paper as the dominant base; one quiet weighty composite hue as the main ink; and a lighter, greyer same-family mist colour when distance needs it.
- Establish hierarchy through colour area, value, and halftone density. Permit one extremely small accent only when it has an explicit narrative role.
- The paper field must remain the largest area, the main ink must carry the anchor and primary boundary, and the mist layer must recede rather than become a second focal point.
- Avoid rainbow palettes, large saturated backgrounds, generic black-plus-red drama, muddy ageing filters, neon glow, and corporate brand systems.

## Copy and typography belong to 009

009 text is an intelligent editorial intervention embedded in the spatial structure—not a headline pasted into blank space, a commercial slogan, UI label, or detached caption block.

- Automatic copy derives one extremely short title from the photograph's subject, emotion, time, action, material, distance, or supported metaphor, plus zero to three micro-elements only when they deepen the same reading.
- Micro-elements may include a tiny note, restrained sentence, chapter mark, catalogue-like code, year, or coordinate-like number, but factual-looking details must be supplied or reliably established. Never invent dates, coordinates, locations, or provenance as facts.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 009 construction:

- **Role:** a compositional axis, boundary marker, interrupted trace, or quiet curatorial annotation.
- **Voice:** abstract, literary, curatorial, restrained, precise, and resonant rather than explanatory or promotional.
- **Construction:** one extremely short title plus one to three micro-elements; use a restrained sans, gentle serif, or minimal condensed face with strong scale contrast, light weight, careful spacing, superscript, small caps, or asymmetric alignment when native to the script.
- **Placement:** run along the horizon, hug the anchor contour, create a vertical axis, cross a halftone boundary, hide in negative space, or participate in controlled overlap, interruption, and alignment. A split word or vertical setting is allowed only when readability survives.
- **Material:** screenprint or letterpress ink with the same grain, slight misregistration, and imperfect coverage as the image.
- **Script:** use a native high-end editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than forcing Latin tracking, rotation, small caps, or vertical logic across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

## Composition commands

```bash
# Source-adaptive top-bottom
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout top-bottom --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

# Source-adaptive left-right
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout left-right --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout left-right

  --out result.png --layout design-only

  --layout design-only --size 1440x3200
```
